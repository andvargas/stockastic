# Resistance Rejection Trading Strategy

## 1. Identify Strong Resistance

- Look for a level where price got rejected multiple times before 
  (horizontal resistance, descending trendline, or major MA like 50/200).
- Higher timeframe resistance (weekly/monthly) = stronger, 
  but we’ll focus on **daily charts**.

---

## 2. Wait for Reversal Signal

At resistance, look for price action confirmation that buyers are failing:

- **Bearish engulfing candle**
- **Shooting star / inverted hammer**
- **Evening star pattern**
- Or simply a daily close back **below resistance** after a failed breakout attempt.

📌 Don’t short just because price touched resistance — wait for sellers to step in.

---

## 3. Check Volume / Momentum

- Reversal candle should have **higher volume** than the prior candles.
- RSI ideally below **70** (showing it’s not in strong breakout mode).
- MACD bearish cross or bearish divergence = extra confirmation.

---

## 4. Entry Trigger

- Enter short on the **next day’s break below the reversal candle’s low**.
- Example: Resistance = 100, reversal candle low = 99 → Place **sell stop** at 98.90.
- This way, you only enter if sellers actually push lower.

---

## 5. Risk Management

- **Stop-loss**: Just above resistance / reversal candle high.  
  Example: Resistance = 100, candle high = 102 → Stop at 102.20.
- **Target**: Nearest support / recent swing low.
- Risk-to-Reward must be **≥ 2:1**.  
  Example: Risk = $2, Target = $4 → ✅ Good trade.

---

## 🔎 Example in Action

- Stock rallies into resistance at **$150**.  
- Prints an **evening star** with high at **152**, closes at **149**.  
- Next day, place **sell stop** at **148.90**.  
- Stop-loss = **152.20**.  
- Target = **140** (previous support).  
- Risk = $3.30, Reward = $8.90 → ✅ 2.7:1 (excellent).

---

⚡ **Pro Tip:**  
Best shorts happen when resistance aligns with **confluence**  
(major fib level, 200 EMA, or weekly/monthly high).
